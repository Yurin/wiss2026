Ultralytics guide-generation dependencies and pretrained models

This project uses Ultralytics for guide image generation and includes the following pretrained model files:
- WISS-Pair/backend/yolov8n.pt
- WISS-Pair/backend/yolov8n-pose.pt
- WISS-Pair/backend/yolo11s-seg.pt

Upstream: https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics
Pretrained assets: https://github.com/ultralytics/assets
